Mindful Insights

Tips

Practical ideas for making movement, routine, and daily self-support easier to begin and easier to repeat.

Tip

Make the First Step Smaller

When motivation is low, the goal is not intensity. The goal is a starting point your body and mind can accept.

Tip

Choose a Minimum Version

Decide what the smallest useful version of movement looks like before the day becomes busy or unpredictable.

Tip

Pair Movement With an Existing Cue

Attach a short movement reset to something already in your day, like making coffee, changing rooms, or finishing work.
